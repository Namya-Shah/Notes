## Headlines

*   **The Anthropic Economic Index (AEI) tracks the evolving role of AI in the global economy.** This index leverages data from Claude conversations to map economic trends related to AI usage. *   **AI adoption is uneven across geography and enterprise size.** The AEI reveals significant differences in how AI technologies are being implemented in the US versus other regions, as well as across different types of businesses. *   **Insights from Claude 3.7 Sonnet are informing the Economic Index.**  Analysis of conversations with this model provides valuable data points for understanding current AI applications and their economic consequences. *   **The Clio system enables privacy-preserving analysis of Claude conversations.** Utilizing a system designed to preserve user privacy is crucial to the development and refinement of the Anthropic Economic Index.
