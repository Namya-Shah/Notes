"A human being should be able to change a diaper, plan an invasion, butcher a hog, conn a ship, design a building, write a sonnet, balance accounts, build a wall, set a bone, comfort the dying, take orders, give orders, cooperate, act alone, solve equations, analyze a new problem, pitch manure, program a computer, cook a tasty meal, fight efficiently, die gallantly. Specialization is for insects." - Robert A Heinlein
- **Specialists**
	- Specialists are defined as a person who concentrates primarily on a particular subject or activity.
	- A person highly skilled in specific field.
	- In other words, a person who has a lot of training, knowledge or skill in a specific area.
	- They are regarded as either experts, or connoisseurs in their field.
- **Generalists**
	- A generalist, on the other hand, is defined as a person who has a wide range of knowledge in various subjects and holds many skillsets.
	- A person whose knowledge, aptitudes, and skills are applied to a field as a whole or to a variety of different fields.
- A *real* generalist will have competent, at least above average skills in their fields.
