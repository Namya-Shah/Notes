Most people think that being attractive is about looking good. And while your looks do matter to some extent, they are not the full picture. Being attractive has a lot more to do with having a charismatic and magnetic personality that draws people in, that makes you unforgettable. And today we’re going to learn exactly how to do that — How to captivate others, build unshakable confidence, and rebrand yourself to create an identity that aligns with the highest version of you.

# Notes
1. Enclothed cognition
	1. Put some amount of effort in your clothing
	2. It is a lot of automatic positive self talk, repeating over and over again in your head
2. The Spike Method
	1. Focus on becoming an interesting person
	2. "You had one thing that defined you & people remembered you for it."
	3. How to find
		1. Figure out 1-2 niche things that truly interest you.
		2. Invest time & energy to become better than the average person at it.
	4. Your spike becomes your way of being introduced in a memorable way.
3. Master the art of conversation
	1. Mastering the art of conversation is easier than you are made to believe.
	2. The goal of a conversation is to connect and not to impress
	3. Be a curious sponge, not being a megaphone
	4. Conversational toolkit up your sleeve
	5. Remember that energy > vocabulary
	6. They always remember how that person made them feel
4. Live an interesting life
	1. "If you want people to be interested in you, you need to have something interesting to offer."
5. Stop being available to people all the time
	1. We value what feels rare & intentional
	2. What needs to be done
		1. Start valuing your time & energy and ask yourself "does this align with what I want".
		2. Start setting boundaries & tell people NO.
6. Cultivate respect
	1. Respect is hard earned
	2. Discipline is that what makes you that person
	3. "If you want a personality that leaves a lasting impression; Start with kindness."
- **Homework**
	- Figure out three ways in which you can enhance your appearance.
	- Figure out one thing that you find interesting and spend 30 minutes a day learning more about that.
	- Practice striking a chat with strangers.
