# Notes
- The secret to being articulate is baked in the Latin word articulus which means small connected parts.
- Take complex big topics they simplify it down to smaller bits, remove the ones that don't really matter and show you the connections between the smaller parts that actually do and they communicate coherently.
- If you tend to ramble, instead of just letting yourself ramble. Start with the sentence the one thing. Already it will help you simplify and prioritize your thoughts.
- If you're not getting anywhere in a meeting, use the framework:
	- Goal
	- Problem
	- Solution
	- Alternatives
	- Trade-offs
- Prerequisite for everything able to use the frameworks is to **pause**.
- Two types of system thinking
	- **SYSTEM 1 THINKING**
		- Fast
		- Gut Feeling
		- Unconscious
		- Words we blurt out
		- Words we resort to
	- **SYSTEM 2 THINKING**
		- More deliberate
		- More analytical
- The 80/20 Rule
	- In a nutshell, it means that there's 20% of work that will give you 80% of results.
