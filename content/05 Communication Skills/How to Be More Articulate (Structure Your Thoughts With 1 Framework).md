# Notes
- Opportunities for team member
	- Find a problem worth solving
	- For the team, the opportunity lies in resolving the pain point that your manager desperately wants to solve.
	- Three points to resolve
		- Figure out which pain point is worth resolving
			- Find out between high value work and low value work
		- Share a plan to resolve the worthwhile pain point
			- S -> Situation
				- Define the "what" and "what next"
				- What is happening now and what is happening in the future.
			- C -> Complication
				- Define the "why"
				- By getting specific, then you can actually articulate what is the real problem, and then you'll find the solution to that real problem.
			- Q -> Question
				- Define the "problem"
			- A -> Answer
				- Define the "solution"
				- Make your answer specific
				- A framework to make your answer specific (SMART):
					- S -> Specific
					- M -> Measurable
					- A -> Achievable
					- R -> Relevant
					- T -> Time-Bound
	- 3 things you need to couch
		- Ask them for their input
		- Couch your plan with a safety net
		- Use Jeff Bezos's the Reversibility Framework
- Opportunities for a manager
	- Coach your team through SCQA
	- Go through "SCQA" yourself
