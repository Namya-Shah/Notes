# Notes
## Why do we Ramble?
1. Lack of Point
2. Lack of Structure
3. Lack of Specificity
## Framework 1: Get to the point
- **Start with:** "The one thing you must know about this topic is..."
- *Use your language to guide your thinking*
## Framework 2: Add Structure
- ### The Three Line Scene
	- **Line 1 (sets the scene):**
		- "The one thing you need to know about this topic is X"
	- **Line 2 (adds depth):**
		- Go Deeper
			- "What I mean by X is..."
		- Introduce a surprise
	- **Line 3 (what's next):**
		- Open the conversation with a question
			- "And the question is..."
		- Close the conversation with an answer
			- "And it's because..."
### Framework 3: Make it Specific
- Via Negativa
	- What it is not, rather than what it is.
