# Notes
- The best ideas don't always speak for themselves.
- Just because you have the best idea, if you can't tell a clear story around it, no one will notice because everyone has great ideas.
- Brevity $\not =$ Concise
- It's not about word count, it's about clarity in the message.
- Recommendation should be the starting point. We should then provide supporting arguments. Then help the listener prioritize by picking out one of the buckets.
- Pyramid Principle
	- Answer
	- Supporting arguments
	- Supporting evidence or data
- One thing
	- Helping them prioritize.
- BLUF
	- Bottom Line Upfront
- Use high information dense words
- You want to spot your vague words and sharpen them with specificity.
	- Reducing use of "some" and "few"
	- You can add depth by just swapping out these vague words for something more specific.
- GPSAT Framework
	- Goal
	- Problem
	- Solution
	- Alternatives
	- Trade-offs
- **Structure what to say first!**
