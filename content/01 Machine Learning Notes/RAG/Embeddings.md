# Brief Introduction
- It means that they can turn something like a video, text, image into a mathematical format.
- Embeddings convert text into dense numerical vectors that capture meaning, relationships, and context -- not just keywords.
- They enable semantic search, allowing retrieval systems to find related ideas even when worded differently.
- Embeddings power vector databases, similarity ranking, and effective context injection for LLMs.
