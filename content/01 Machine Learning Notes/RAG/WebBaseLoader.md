# Brief Introduction
- **WebBaseLoader lets you load live webpage content**, perfect for real-world RAG systems where data isn't limited to local files.
- It fetches any URL, extracts the readable text, cleans it, and returns structured Document objects.
- Eliminates the chaos of manual copy-paste or messy web formatting -- everything becomes clean and pipeline-ready.
- Enables powerful use cases like Q&A over webpages, news summarization, product comparison bots, and Nepali market research assistants.
- Makes it effortless to turn a live webpage (like a TechLekh article) into a fully functional LLM-powered Q&A system in just a few steps.
