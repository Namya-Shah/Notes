# Brief Introduction
Sequence-to-sequence learning (Seq2Seq) is about training models to convert sequences from one domain to sequences in another domain.

> [!QUESTION]
> Difference between sampling rate and frequency?
> A. Sampling rate refers to how often a signal is measured (samples per second), while frequency refers to how many cycles of a wave occur per second.

- **Audio Speech Recognition (ASR)**
	- Sampling rate -> Tokens/Sequence of words
- **Text to Speech (TTS)**
	- Tokens/Sequence of words -> Sampling rate
