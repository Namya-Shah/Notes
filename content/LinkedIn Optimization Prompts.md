| Title                                   | Prompt                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The Brutal Profile Roast**            | Here is my full LinkedIn profile. Read it like a recruiter who sees 200 profiles a day. Tell me in a numbered list: what makes you skip it, what sounds copied from someone else, what keywords are missing for [my target role], and what you would change first. Be specific, not generic. |
| **Headline That Gets You Found**        | Rewrite my LinkedIn headline. Rules: no words like 'passionate' or 'results-driven'. Include my target role, one specific skill, and who I help. Give me 5 versions in a table with a column explaining why each one works. Here is my current headline: [PASTE]                             |
| **About Section That Hooks in 2 Lines** | Rewrite my About section. The first 2 lines must make someone click 'see more'. Include: what I do, one specific result I have delivered, and who I do it for. Keep it under 200 words. No corporate language. Here is my current About: [PASTE]                                             |
| **Turn Job Duties into Achievements**   | Here is my Experience section. Every bullet is a job duty, not an achievement. For each one, rewrite it with a number or result. If there is no number, ask me a follow-up question to find one. Format: show the before and after side by side.                                             |
| **Steal Keywords From Job Posts**       | Here are 3 job postings for the role I want: [PASTE]. Pull out every keyword that appears more than once. Now check my profile and tell me which keywords I am missing. Add them naturally into my headline, about and experience without keyword stuffing.                                  |
| **Featured Section That Sells You**     | Based on my profile and target role, tell me exactly what 3 things I should pin in my Featured section to make recruiters message me. For each one, tell me: what it should be (a post, a project link, a PDF, a video), what it should say, and why it works.                               |
| **One-Week Content Plant**              | Based on my profile and the role I want, give me a 7-day LinkedIn posting plan. One post per day. Each post should have a hook line, the type of post (story, opinion, carousel, poll), and how it positions me as an expert. Make every post something I can write in 15 minutes.           |
