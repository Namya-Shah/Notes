# Notes
- If you want to be a great leader, you also want to be a great communicator
- You want to communicate with energy, clarity and empathy
- Are we losing communication skills in this digital world?
    - The answer unfortunately is yes
    - The level of digital consumption is going up, the level of empathy is going down
- The time you spend on your bed being lazy you lose time on developing your social skills
- We are more and more impatient
- We are constantly interrupted
- Adding more and more communication channels doesn’t mean it will add more quality of communication
- We should reclaim and focus on analog communication
- You cannot “not” communicate
- You cannot say what you mean
- You make people feel what you want them to feel
- First impressions last
- Be interested rather than being interesting
- You don’t look at the phone while talking to someone
- Your body speaks louder than words
- Non verbal communication can cause problems and you need to see people for at least 3 seconds before changing your view to other person
