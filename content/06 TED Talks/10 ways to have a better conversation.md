- # Notes
- She speaks about how people these days talk to their friends through text rather than talking to them face to face
- She refers to the Atlantic that was written by a high school professor in which he wrote “I came to realise that conversational competence might be the single most overlooked skill we fail to teach”
- Kids spend hours each day engaging with ideas and each other through screens, but rarely do they have the opportunity to hone their interpersonal communication skills
- 10 Skills
	- Don’t multitask (be present)
	- Don’t pontificate
	- Use open-ended questions
		- Who, what, why, where
	- Go with the flow
	- If you don’t know, say that you don’t know
	- Don’t equate your experience with theirs
		- Don’t tell them that same experience happened to you
	- Try not to repeat yourself
		- Do not repeat yourself over and over again
	- Stay out of the weeds
		- People don’t care about date, names or any other details
	- Listen
		- Listening is the perhaps the most important skill you need
		- Steven Covey said “Most of us don’t listen with the intent to understand, we listen with the intent to reply”
	- Be brief
		- A good conversation is like a miniskirt; short enough to retain interest, but long enough to cover the subject.
		- Be interested in other people
- Always be prepared to be amazed and you will never be disappointed
- Go out, talk to people, listen to people, and most importantly be prepared to be amazed
