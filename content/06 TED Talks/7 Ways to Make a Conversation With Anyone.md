# Notes
- Every time you talk to a stranger a metal link is formed
- Every conversation you have with that person the link gets stronger and stronger
- Everyday each one of us meet so many stranger for e.g., grocery guy, cab guy
- A conversation gives you a whole new perspective and opens a whole new door
- A conversation can cause war or create peace
- **The First Word Flood Gates**
    - Every conversation with a stranger comes with an opportunity
    - Talk to them and the worst that can happen is they won’t talk still they are not talking to you
- **Skip The Small Talk**
    - Don’t talk same old questions
    - Ask personal questions
    - If you ask then the other person is willing to share
- **Find The Me-Toos**
    - Rather than being negative, find me toos in both of you
- **Pay A Unique Compliment**
    - You don’t have to lie
    - Pay a unique and genuine compliment
- **Ask For An Opinion**
    - Everyone needs a validation
    - Asking opinion makes you know more about the other people
    - Keep it generic
- **Be Present**
    - When someone’s trying to communicate, the least you can do is make eye contact and listen them carefully
- **Name, Place, Animal, Thing**
    - Remember their name or place or animal or thing

A conversation is like reading a book, you can turn to any chapter you want.
