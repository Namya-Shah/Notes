# Notes
- Adolf Hitler is an example of communicate as a leader
- Slides isn’t about number of slides
- It also isn’t about the style
- It’s about how you present to world
- The presentation with your manager is not that much important than he telling the board of directors your presentation
- Retell-ability or representability is very much important
- The Pyramid of Planned Outcome
    - Action (Decision)
        - Action is preceded by belief
    - Beliefs (Big Ideas)
- Communication is vital so we need to be aware of the 3 sirens
- Retell-ability is the standard to aim for and it is the key that will unlock everything else
- The pyramid is the tool that will get you there
