Never try to scale or assume yourself same as Google as there are different problems we face when going to FAANG companies.

For small scale projects, don't try to become someone who wants to make Google today but take baby steps to become an equivalent to Google.

> *"You don't need a factory to make a sandwich"*

You don't need enterprise level abstractions, you need speed, clarity and brevity.
> **Ship fast, fix fast, learn faster**
