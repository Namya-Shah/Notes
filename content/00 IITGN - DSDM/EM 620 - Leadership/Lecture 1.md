# Does Innvoativeness improve Competitiveness?
- Competitiveness is the desire to win, to stand out, to get ahead.
- Innovativeness (as we shall see) is about finding new and different means to get to an objective.
- Innovative ideas, because no one else has thought of them, immediately bestow a competitive advantage on you.
# What is Innovativeness?
- Innovativeness is applying creative thinking for
	- Creating a *new* product, service or process, **OR**
	- Making an *existing* product, service or process *more* beneficial
# Hurdles to Innovativeness
- Thinking up something ***new*** is *not always easy*.
- Doing something new involves more ***risk***
- Subconscious belief that there is only one, regular way to do something
- Not recognizing that innovativeness is almost always an option.
- ***Inertia*** against trying something new
