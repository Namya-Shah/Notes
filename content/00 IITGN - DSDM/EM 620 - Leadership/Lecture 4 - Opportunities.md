# Assessing Opportunities
Opportunities are commonly discarded because:
- They look too difficult, or time-consuming, or expensive - to do or even look into. Not worth all the effort.
- We instinctively see all the risks, but don't always see all the benefits at first glance.
# Key Takeaways
- Rather than leaving opportunities, try to grab as much opportunities as possible.
- Failures are not a wall for you to stop there but just an obstacle to jump from
